# Contributing

1. Use official vendor sources and record the review date.
2. Add the exact credential title under its technology, role or level.
3. Link the credential-specific page where possible. Record whether it is active, beta, upcoming, retired or unverified.
4. Keep exams, certificates, combined designations, microcredentials and certifications distinct.
5. Reconcile all pages of a catalogue before claiming completeness. Explain inaccessible or gated sections in COVERAGE.md.
6. Update the README entry counts after changes. Do not add pricing or renewal claims without dated official evidence.
