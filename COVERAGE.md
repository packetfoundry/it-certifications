## Completion criteria

A vendor is fully reconciled only after all current public catalogue pages and pagination have been compared, every credential has an official credential-specific source, beta/upcoming/retired records are distinguished, and exams are separated from awarded certifications. Do not remove coverage limitations until those checks are complete.
